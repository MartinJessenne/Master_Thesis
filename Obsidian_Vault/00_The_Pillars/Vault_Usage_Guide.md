Welcome back to your Master's Thesis vault. The structure has been optimized to match your `Master_Thesis.typ` and support an iterative research loop.

## 1. The Research Loop
To keep your vault organized, follow this flow:
1. **Inquiry:** Start a new question in `10_Inquiry/` (e.g., "What if we change the step size?").
2. **Logic:** Define the math or code needed in `20_Logic_and_Method/`.
3. **Trial:** Log your experiment run in `30_Trials/`.
4. **Evidence:** Interpret the results and plots in `40_Evidence/`.

## 2. Navigation Hubs
- **[[Thesis_Master_Plan]]**: Your top-level roadmap.
- **[[Master_Dashboard]]**: A live view of all your active inquiries and findings (requires Dataview).
- **[[Research_Flow.canvas]]**: A visual whiteboard to map your hypotheses.

## 3. Using Templates
When creating a new note, use the **Templates** (`Alt+E` by default) to ensure correct metadata:
- `T_Inquiry`: For new research questions.
- `T_Trial`: For logging experiment parameters.
- `T_Evidence`: For analyzing results.
- `T_Algorithm_Pseudocode`: For professional math logic blocks.

## 4. Automation Tips
- **Backlinks:** Always set the `related_pillar` property in the YAML header to the relevant Chapter Pillar (e.g., `"[[Ch4_Empirical_Study]]"`).
- **Graph View:** Filter by `tag:#chapter_4` to see only the notes related to your empirical study.

Good luck with your research!
