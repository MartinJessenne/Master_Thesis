# Obsidian Vault Context

This directory is a Zettelkasten containing the notes, literature, and planning for the Master's thesis.

## Structure and Organization
- **00_The_Pillars**: Core chapters and master plans.
- **10_Inquiry**: Questions, hypotheses, and theoretical investigations.
- **20_Logic_and_Method**: Explanations of logic, implementation details, methodologies, and architectural breakdowns.
- **30_Trials**: Records of experiments and runs.
- **40_Evidence**: Results, convergence metrics, and findings from trials.
- **90_Library**: Literature notes (e.g., summaries of papers).
- **99_System**: Templates for different types of notes.

## Workflow and Rules
- Maintain the Zettelkasten organization by placing new notes in the appropriate folders.
- Use Obsidian syntax, particularly `[[backlinks]]`, to connect related concepts and notes.
- When explaining complex concepts or providing detailed answers, generate a highly contextualized and detailed Obsidian `.md` file in this vault rather than dumping long text in the chat. Use the `obsidian-note` skill for this.
